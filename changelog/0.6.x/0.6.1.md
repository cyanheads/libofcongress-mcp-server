# 0.6.1 — 2026-11-13

## Added

- **Document transcriptions** in `libofcongress_get_newspaper_page`: a page with no text service of its own reads its resource's `fulltext_file` when that is an `https://tile.loc.gov/` URL whose path ends `.xml`, and returns the `<tei2>` transcription of the whole document as `ocr_text`, with `text_scope: "document"` (`"page"` on every page with a text service of its own) and `text_attribution`; `match` scans it, and `libofcongress_get_item` marks such resources `has_transcription`. Manuscript editors' notes and canceled wording are left out. ([#90](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/90))
- **`include_facets` and `language`** on `libofcongress_search_newspapers`: `newspaper_title_facets`, `state_facets`, and `language_facets` list LOC's top values, each accepted unchanged by the filter it names, and rows carry `languages`. ([#91](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/91))
- **`contributor` filter** on `libofcongress_search_items`, with `contributor_facets` under `include_facets`, and `contributor_terms` (the filter's form) on rows LOC credits and on `libofcongress_get_item`; rendered text joins them, and `libofcongress_get_item`'s `contributors`, with ` | `. ([#92](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/92))

## Changed

- **`request_timed_out` recovery** on `libofcongress_search_items`, `libofcongress_search_newspapers`, `libofcongress_browse_collections`, `libofcongress_get_item`, `libofcongress_get_newspaper_page`, and the `libofcongress://item/{+item_id}` resource leads with one unchanged retry, and the entry is declared `retryable: true`; `libofcongress_search_subjects` is unchanged. ([#89](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/89))

## Fixed

- **HTTP 520 and non-ceiling 400** from LOC fail as `upstream_unavailable` instead of reading as empty or out-of-range results; a 400 means the retrieval ceiling only on a page past it. ([#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/86))
- **Resources without a `url`** (recordings, tape sides, reels) are listed by `libofcongress_get_item` with `page_count: 0`; earlier versions dropped them. `resources[].url` is now optional, and every resource carries `video` and `download_restricted` when LOC sends them. Protocol- and root-relative related-item links and resource `url`, `image`, `pdf`, `audio`, and `video` links resolve to absolute URLs. ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/87))
- **Compact sort dates** (`18631100`, `19660000`) read at their own precision (`1863-11`, `1966`) in search rows and `libofcongress_get_item`; a year of `0000` is left unconverted. ([#88](https://github.com/cyanheads/libofcongress-mcp-server/issues/88))
- **A `|` in a filter value** (`subject`, `location`, and `contributor` on `libofcongress_search_items`; `state`, `newspaper_title`, and `language` on `libofcongress_search_newspapers`): a `|` or spaces at either end are dropped and the search runs; a `|` between other characters, or a value with nothing else in it, is refused with `invalid_filter_value` before any request, since LOC reads `|` as a separator between filters.
- **HTTP 414** from loc.gov on `libofcongress_search_items`, `libofcongress_search_newspapers`, and `libofcongress_browse_collections` is reported as `query_too_long` (non-retryable) rather than `upstream_unavailable`.
